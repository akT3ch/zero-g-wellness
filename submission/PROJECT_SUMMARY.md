# Project summary

Astronauts traveling far from Earth may have to interpret health changes before ground support can respond. Zero-G Wellness combines daily check-ins, transparent individual baseline comparisons and NASA space weather context in a browser-based research prototype. A reproducible Mars transit lets judges follow sleep disruption, cardiovascular change, concurrent stress and fatigue deviations, and gradual recovery. Each alert shows the measurements and thresholds behind it. An independent research workspace connects the demonstration to NASA open science resources.

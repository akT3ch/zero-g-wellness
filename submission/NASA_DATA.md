# NASA data and resources

- [NASA OSDR](https://osdr.nasa.gov/) supports open study discovery, metadata review and accessible research file analysis.
- [NASA CCMC DONKI](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/tools/DONKI/) supplies reported solar flares and solar energetic particle events for the environment panel.
- [NASA Human Research Program](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/) provides context for why long-duration crews monitor sleep, behavioral health and physiological adaptation.
- [NOAA SWPC](https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/products/goes-proton-flux) supplies GOES proton flux. The mission simulation uses authored synthetic readings and its own scripted event.

See [data provenance](../docs/DATA_SOURCES.md) for measurement types, verification windows and limitations.
